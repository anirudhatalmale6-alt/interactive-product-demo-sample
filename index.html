    01

    It reads the soil, not the forecast.

    Three probes per block, at 20, 40 and 60 centimetres. You see where the water actually is — not where an app guessed it might be. Fourteen days of history sit behind every number.

    • 90-second sampling
    • Battery life measured in seasons
    • Works offline, syncs when it can

    02

    Every block, at a glance.

    The map is the index. Thirsty parcels warm up, healthy ones stay quiet, and you can go from twenty-four blocks to one root zone in a single tap.

    • Colour tracks deficit, not rainfall
    • Drawn from your own boundary file
    • Tap a parcel to try it

    03

    A plan you can argue with.

    TERRA proposes tonight’s windows and the draw they’ll cost. Switch a zone off and the total moves with you — nothing is locked until you say so.

    • Per-zone override
    • Live cost in cubic metres
    • Pushes straight to your controller

    04

    Set the rule. See it back-tested.

    Drag the threshold and TERRA replays the last fortnight against it, so you know how noisy a rule is before it starts waking you at four in the morning.

    • Instant back-test
    • Quiet hours respected
    • SMS, push, or nothing at all

    05

    Paperwork that writes itself.

    Water-use reporting is a legal chore. TERRA keeps the audit trail as it goes and hands you the finished document on the first of the month.

    • PDF, CSV or XLSX
    • Signed and timestamped
    • Five years of retention

    About this page

    This is a sample, built to be looked at closely.

    TERRA is invented — the product, the numbers, the grower. Everything else is real: hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework, no build step, no third-party requests. The fonts are self-hosted and subset, the charts and the map are inline SVG drawn from data at runtime, and the whole page weighs a little over a hundred kilobytes.

    • StackVanilla HTML5 / CSS3 / ES6 — drop it on any host
    • Weight103 KB total, 7 requests, no CDN
    • NavigationScroll, click the rail, or use ← →
    • Responsive320 px phone to 4K, one codebase
    • AccessReal buttons, focus rings, reduced-motion respected
    • EmbedWhole page, or one section in an iframe

    Swap the copy, the palette and the five panels for your product and this becomes your demo.
